1. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you create an account and build your profile, we collect:

  • Account information: your email address and a password (your password is handled and stored securely by our authentication provider; we never see it in plain text).
  • Profile information: your display name, a short “about me” bio, your date of birth (used to calculate and display your age), your gender, the genders you’re interested in being shown, and free-form interest tags (for example “Gaming”, “Anime”, “Cozy nights in”).
  • Photos: the profile photos you choose to upload.
  • Communications: messages you send to us, such as support or feedback emails.

Information generated through your use of the Service

  • Activity & matching data: the people you like or pass on, your matches, and the connections those swipes create.
  • Desirability score: we calculate an internal, relative score (and counts of likes and passes received) that helps us decide the order in which profiles are shown. This is computed automatically on our servers and is not displayed to other users.
  • Technical & usage data: information generated automatically when you use the app, such as approximate timestamps, app and device information, and diagnostic data from the platforms and tools we use to keep the Service running.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • create and manage your account and authenticate you;
  • build and show your profile, and show you other people’s profiles;
  • power matching — including filtering candidates by your stated preferences and ordering the discover deck using the desirability score;
  • screen and moderate uploaded photos before they are shown;
  • operate, maintain, secure, debug, and improve the Service;
  • respond to your support requests and communicate with you about the Service;
  • detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, safety issues, and violations of our Terms of Service; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

3. How we share information

We are not in the business of selling your personal data. We share information only as described below:

  • With other users: your profile is meant to be seen. Your display name, age, gender, bio, interests, and approved photos are visible to other users as part of normal use of the Service. When you match with someone, you become visible to each other as a match. Please only put information in your profile that you’re comfortable sharing with other people.
  • With service providers: we share information with the vendors that help us run the Service (see Service providers below).
  • For legal reasons: we may disclose information if we believe it is reasonably necessary to comply with a law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; to enforce our Terms; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of After Hours, our users, or others.
  • In a business transfer: if After Hours is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

4. Service providers

We use Google Firebase (provided by Google LLC) as our backend infrastructure. Depending on the feature, this includes Firebase Authentication (account sign-in), Cloud Firestore (storing your profile and activity data), Cloud Storage (storing your photos), Cloud Functions (server-side processing such as match and score calculations), and a Firebase image-processing extension that resizes and screens uploaded photos. These providers process information on our behalf so we can operate the Service. Google’s handling of data is described in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Photos & moderation

When you upload a photo, the original is stored and then automatically processed: it is resized for display and screened by an automated moderation step. Only photos that pass moderation are shown to other users; photos that are blocked are not displayed. This screening helps keep explicit or disallowed content off the Service. Automated moderation isn’t perfect, and we may also remove content in response to reports.

6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we need to retain certain information to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or abuse, or enforce our agreements. Note that content you have already shared with other users (for example, in a match) may persist with them.

7. Your rights & choices

You can review and update most of your profile information at any time directly in the app. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your personal information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where we rely on it; and
  • request a copy of your information in a portable format.

To exercise these rights, use the in-app controls where available or contact us at info@piedmontsys.com. We will respond consistent with applicable law, and we may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from within the app or by emailing info@piedmontsys.com. Deleting your account removes your profile from the Service and deletes or de-identifies your personal information, subject to the retention exceptions described above.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls on our backend. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please help protect your account by using a strong, unique password and keeping it confidential.

10. Children’s privacy

After Hours is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us at info@piedmontsys.com and we will take steps to delete it.

11. International data transfers

We and our service providers may store and process your information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Those countries may have data-protection laws that differ from those where you live. Where required, we take steps to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection when it is transferred.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where the changes are significant, provide additional notice (such as an in-app message). We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.

13. Contact us

If you have any questions or requests about this Privacy Policy or your information, contact us at info@piedmontsys.com.
